Why this journal

01 / POSITION

The internet is not short of opinions about AI. I am not trying to fix that.

I started chizh.us for a more selfish reason: my own perception keeps changing. Tools become collaborators, interfaces start steering judgment, and yesterday's weird behavior becomes today's default. If I do not write it down, the changes collapse into one endless product launch.

This journal is how I slow that stream down, keep my bearings, and leave a record I can argue with later.

I keep returning to what happens to judgment when generation becomes cheap; where delegation ends and responsibility starts; how interfaces teach us to trust, comply, or stop noticing; and what prolonged use does to attention, ambition, taste, and the feeling that a piece of work is still yours.
