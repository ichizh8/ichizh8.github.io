A laboratory journal, not a content channel

chizh.us is Iurii Chizh's English-language journal about how people think, choose, create, cooperate, and remain accountable when intelligence is shared with machines.

The object

It studies neither humans nor AI in isolation, but the arrangements through which they perceive, influence, delegate to, and depend on one another. Research, history, interfaces, companies, and products appear as evidence and counterexamples, not as content marketing.

The primary object of a piece is an argument, model, distinction, or question worth preserving. Cases support the argument; they do not dictate the publishing agenda. Not every piece ends in a corporate checklist. A sharper model of reality is a legitimate output.

Traditions

Four, used as lenses rather than as silos.

Decision science
Choice, uncertainty, expertise, evidence, preference, and error.
Human-computer interaction
Interfaces, affordances, mental models, cognitive load, coordination, and situated use.
The psychology of human-AI interaction
Trust, reliance, calibration, anthropomorphism, attachment, persuasion, identity, and behavioral adaptation around intelligent systems.
Organizational and institutional theory
Delegation, authority, accountability, incentives, memory, legitimacy, and collective action.

Human-AI psychology is useful shorthand but not the public category: it implies a settled discipline that does not yet exist as one coherent field. The preferred phrase isthe psychology of human-AI interaction, or better, the specific mechanism being discussed.

Formats

Five. Subjects are not formats.

Essay
Develop an authored thesis, distinction, or conceptual model. One consequential tension, a defensible thesis, clear definitions, relevant evidence, at least one concrete case or counterexample, and one serious objection.
Field Note
Use a bounded observation to refine a larger idea. States whose behavior was observed, under what conditions, what interpretation it supports, what alternative remains possible, and why it cannot automatically be generalized.
Applied Research Note
Turn research into an authored interpretation or testable implication. It fails if the reader learns about a paper but cannot see the author’s intellectual position.
Decision Memo
Make a consequential theoretical or practical choice legible: situation, decision owner, viable options, evidence and assumptions, trade-offs and reversibility, recommendation, and the conditions that would change it.
Diagram
Compress a useful conceptual model without pretending that arrows are evidence. One named model, defined elements and relationships, instructions for use, boundary conditions, and a version.

Evidence

Every factual public sentence is classified before publication as an authored judgment, a public fact, a research finding, a personal or product operating fact, an employer or client claim, a metric claim, or a causal claim. The last three default todeny without exact permission, method, and attribution.

Citations sit next to the sentence they support rather than in a pile at the end. For research claims the journal records what was measured, in what population, under what comparison, and with what material limitations. Laboratory findings do not silently become social laws, and reported trust is not observed reliance.

If a source cannot support the precise wording, the wording is narrowed or the claim is removed. Confidence is not a citation format.

Cases and disclosure

A case earns space through explanatory value, not proximity to the author. Every case has to answer one question: what does this reveal that the argument alone would not?

Atrum.dev is a separate studio and commercial object, not the journal's organizing frame. It gets no permanent navigation item, homepage block, editorial quota, or automatic call to action. It may appear when a specific case genuinely illuminates an argument, under the same evidence and permission rules as any other company, and the author's affiliation is disclosed plainly when it does.

Not here

  • AI news summaries and model-release commentary without a durable human question
  • Generic productivity advice, prompt collections, and tool rankings
  • Speculative claims about consciousness presented as settled analysis
  • Therapy or clinical advice
  • Single-study universal psychology
  • Corporate checklists with no underlying theory
  • Claims that humans remain valuable by moral entitlement rather than demonstrated function
  • Content generated because a calendar contains an empty square
