Themes

Five public themes, deliberately fewer than the internal commissioning map. Mind primarily informs Interaction and Judgment. Culture cuts across all five. Evidence and proof are editorial disciplines everywhere, not a separate subject silo.

  1. 01Interaction

    How interfaces shape the relationship before either side appears to make a decision.

    5 entries

  2. 02Judgment

    How people and machines frame, generate, select, verify, reject, and release consequential outputs.

    4 entries

  3. 03Agency

    How initiative and action move across people, models, tools, and organizations.

    4 entries

  4. 04Systems

    How teams and organizations think, coordinate, remember, and fail with intelligent tools.

    3 entries

  5. 05Institutions

    How legitimacy, authority, and remedy are arranged when institutions act through intelligent systems.

    1 entry

What a theme is for

A theme is a place to file an argument, not a content category to be fed. An entry belongs to the journal when it contains a human tension, a machine-mediated arrangement, a mechanism that explains why behavior or outcomes change, and a transferable idea that outlives the specific product release.

Entries carry one or two primary themes and may carry secondary ones. Both are listed on every entry, and both are filterable in the journal index.
