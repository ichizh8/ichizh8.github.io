Theme 04

Systems

How teams and organizations think, coordinate, remember, and fail with intelligent tools.

Scope

Incentives, feedback, coordination, distributed cognition, and failure.

A group with intelligent tools is a cognitive system, not a set of individuals who happen to share software. What it can notice, challenge, remember, and correct is a property of its arrangement.

Work filed here examines feedback, measurement, and coordination as the machinery that decides whether a local improvement survives contact with the rest of the organization.

Objects of study

  • Distributed cognition
  • Decision rights
  • Organizational memory
  • Coordination and dissent
  • Incentives and measurement
  • Feedback, correlated failure, and recovery
  • Escalation paths
  • Monitoring after deployment
