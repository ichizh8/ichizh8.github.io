Theme 05

Institutions

How legitimacy, authority, and remedy are arranged when institutions act through intelligent systems.

Scope

Legitimacy, governance, responsibility, power, memory, and adaptation.

Prediction estimates an outcome. Authority identifies who may impose a decision. No reliability score converts the first into the second.

Work filed here concerns the arrangements that decide who may act, which losses are permissible, how an affected person can challenge a result, and who must correct it afterward.

Objects of study

  • Institutional legitimacy
  • Authority to impose a decision
  • Governance, liability, and correction
  • Contestability and remedy
  • Power and labor
  • Rights and permissible losses
  • Institutional memory
  • Adaptation and reform
